INFS3701 Enterprise Architecture for Scalable Cloud Solutions Group Assignment Marking Scheme Term 3, 2026   ·   Total: 25 marks   ·   Five criteria, 5 marks each Criteria Below Expectations Meeting Expectations Above Expectations  Missing Attempt Just Fully Just Fully Criterion 1: The deployed solution on AWS fulfils the assignment requirements.  (5 marks) The solution does not fulfil the expectations, for example, it runs on a single instance with no load distribution, is not resilient across Availability Zones, or does not scale with demand. The solution mainly fulfils the expectations, load is distributed across multiple instances (or a serverless equivalent), the solution spans multiple Availability Zones and survives failure, and it scales with demand. The solution completely fulfils the core AND goes further with well-chosen enhancements demonstrated to work, for example proven auto scaling or failover, monitoring, caching/CDN, hardened security, or infrastructure-as-code, with choices that hold up to scrutiny. The group does not express (or explain where necessary) aims, ideas and information clearly and precisely in language appropriate for the audience. Required elements (objective; architecture; scalability; high availability; networking/security/data; cost; contribution statement) are missing or unclear, expression/grammar is often inaccurate, and referencing is missing, inaccurate, or mixes more than one style. 
Generally, but not consistently, the group expresses the aims, ideas and information clearly and precisely in appropriate language. The required elements are addressed and the choices are justified. Expression/grammar is generally accurate, the document is professional, with accurate citations in a single, consistent style. AND it is submitted as a PDF within the limits. 
The group consistently expresses aims, arguments and information clearly, precisely and concisely. All required elements are addressed and the design choices are justified with sound reasoning. Expression/grammar is fluent, the document is very professional with accurate referencing in a single, consistent style, and sources are integrated effectively. (5 marks) The diagram is missing or unclear, or does not represent a scalable and highly available architecture; main components, Availability Zones or the data tier are absent or wrong, or it exceeds the page limit. The diagram clearly shows the main components, the Availability Zones, how requests are distributed, and the data tier, within 2 pages as a PDF. The diagram is professional, precise and complete: conveying the scalable, highly available design at a glance with correct AWS notation and no ambiguity. (5 marks) The cost estimate is missing or incomplete, is not based on us-east-1 over a 12-month period, or its assumptions are unclear; or it exceeds the page limit. The cost estimate covers the solution's main services for 12 months in us-east-1 with reasonable, stated assumptions, within 2 pages as a PDF. The cost estimate is thorough and well-justified: all significant services included, assumptions clearly stated, and figures tied directly to the architecture; it demonstrates genuine cost-aware thinking. Marking checklist Core: the expected level (Criterion 1, Meeting) 
☑ Load is distributed across multiple instances (or a serverless service). 
☑ The solution spans multiple Availability Zones and survives the loss of an instance or AZ. 
☑ Capacity scales with demand (auto scaling or serverless). 
☑ A sensible VPC exposes only what must be public; persistent data lives in a resilient store; the interface remains usable. Above Expectations (Criterion 1, full marks) 
☑ Well-chosen enhancements beyond the baseline, demonstrated to work. Documents (Criteria 2–4) 
☑ Design document: objective; architecture; scalability; high availability; networking/security/data; cost; contribution statement. All present; under 4,000 words and 8 pages; PDF; one consistent referencing style. 
☑ Architecture diagram: components, Availability Zones, load distribution and data tier shown clearly; under 2 pages; PDF. 
☑ Cost estimate: 12 months in us-east-1, built with the Pricing Calculator, assumptions stated; under 2 pages; PDF. Video & teamwork (Criterion 5 + contribution) 
☑ Video: solution shown and its scalability/availability demonstrated; key decisions explained; up to two minutes; professional. 
☑ Every member contributed (individual per member); the contribution statement is signed off (individual marks may be scaled for materially uneven contribution).
