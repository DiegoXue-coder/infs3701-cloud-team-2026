Hi everyone!

I'm Tomas Lagos, and I'll be your course convenor and lecturer for INFS3701 Enterprise Architecture for Scalable Cloud Solutions. I'm looking forward to meeting you all next week and building some cloud together!

This course runs in Lecture + Tutorial mode. Each week there's a lecture for the concepts, the business context and real-world cases, and a tutorial where we build things hands-on. Please bring a laptop to every tutorial.



📌 Getting Started

To help you hit the ground running, please have a look at the official course outline:

👉 https://www.unsw.edu.au/course-outlines/course-outline#year=2026&term=Term%203&deliveryMode=In%20Person&deliveryFormat=Standard&teachingPeriod=T3&deliveryLocation=Kensington&courseCode=INFS3701&activityGroupId=1

Two sections in particular:

* Course Schedule: the week-by-week topics, so you can see how the term is structured and which weeks matter most.

* Assessments: what each piece of work involves, what it's worth and when it's due. In short: a weekly participation mark, an individual assignment due in Week 5, a group assignment due in Week 10, and the final exam.



☁️ Your AWS Environment

Everything we build runs in the AWS Academy Learner Lab, which you'll set up in the very first tutorial. A few key points:

* You get US$50 of AWS credits for the term, so it costs you nothing, but the credits are finite. Build, capture your evidence, then tear your resources down.

* Each week has an AWS Academy module knowledge check and/or lab to complete at home before your tutorial. These count towards your participation mark.

* Completing every module, and going on to earn the AWS certification, is entirely optional. You are not disadvantaged in this course if you choose not to.



🎯 Our First Week

In the first lecture we'll cover the foundations: what the cloud actually is, the deployment and service models, and why organisations move to it. In the first tutorial we'll set up your Learner Lab, meet the demo service we'll build on for the first half of the term, and practise prompting an AI assistant. Your individual assignment is released that day, so you'll leave the session knowing exactly what you're working towards.
